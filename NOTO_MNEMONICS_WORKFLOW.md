# Noto Emoji source workflow

Keep the complete Google Noto SVG catalogue only as a LOCAL source library:

`source/noto-svg/`

Do not commit that full catalogue. It is ignored by Git.

After we choose the final mnemonic icons, copy only the selected/normalized SVGs into:

`assets/mnemonics/`

Only `assets/mnemonics/` belongs in the published CW Studio site.
