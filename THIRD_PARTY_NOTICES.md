# Third-party visual assets

CW Studio mnemonic geometry uses selected SVG artwork from **Google Noto Emoji**.

The complete upstream Noto catalogue is not part of CW Studio. Only the curated
mnemonic subset used by the application is published under `assets/mnemonics/`.

Upstream project:
https://github.com/googlefonts/noto-emoji

Noto Emoji artwork is distributed by Google under its upstream licensing terms.
Keep the upstream license/attribution information with redistributed assets.
