## Mnemonic changes made to fit strong Noto assets

- **F · ES** — Faro → Fuego: no dedicated lighthouse emoji in the standard Noto set.
- **L · EN** — Lighthouse → Leaf: clean exact Noto asset.
- **Q · EN** — Quill → Queen: no dedicated quill emoji; princess/queen silhouette is immediately recognizable.
- **X · ES** — No dedicated xylophone emoji; keyboard is the closest clean musical-key visual.
- **X · EN** — Xylophone → X-ray: dedicated exact Noto asset.
- **Z · EN** — Zipper → Zebra: dedicated, instantly recognizable Noto asset.
- **7 · ES** — Hoz → Palo de hockey: clearer Noto asset and strong 7-like silhouette.
- **7 · EN** — Scythe → Hockey stick: clearer Noto asset and strong 7-like silhouette.
